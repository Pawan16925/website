How to Split PDF Files Safely

Simply drop your PDF document into the designated file area above. Once the document information loads, select your extraction method. You can extract a simple start-to-end continuous page range, split every single page into distinct files inside a single ZIP bundle, or specify a customized page formula (such as 1, 3, 5-8). When satisfied, trigger the split process to instantly compile your PDF files locally.
