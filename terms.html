About StaxTech

StaxTech is an online internship platform that offers project-based learning opportunities for students and professionals in various domains like Web Development, Python Programming, Data Science, Digital Marketing, and more. Our model is simple: you enroll, receive a project, complete it independently, and receive a certificate on successful submission and verification.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Anyone above the age of 14 is eligible to apply
  • No prior experience or academic qualification is necessary
  • Must have basic internet access, a device (computer or smartphone), and an an active email ID

Internship Process

  • Step 1: Select your preferred domain from the available options
  • Step 2: Pay a one-time non-refundable enrollment fee of ₹19
  • Step 3: Receive your internship project link and instructions via email or WhatsApp
  • Step 4: Complete the project independently within the deadline (usually 5–15 days)
  • Step 5: Pay ₹69 to receive your certificate after project review

Payment Terms

All payments made to StaxTech are final and non-refundable. We use secure payment gateways. Once a payment is confirmed, no cancellation or reversal is permitted.

  • Registration fee of ₹19 covers internship material access
  • Certificate fee of ₹69 is due after project verification
  • We are not liable for transaction issues due to technical problems

Certification Process

Certificates are awarded only to those who complete the assigned project and pay the final fee of ₹69. Each certificate includes:

  • Your full name and internship domain
  • Project duration and completion date
  • Unique certificate ID for online verification
  • Digital delivery via email or WhatsApp

Note: Physical certificates are not provided. Projects with plagiarism or poor quality will be rejected.

Refund Policy

StaxTech maintains a strict no-refund policy for our digital services.

  • No refunds for registration or certificate fees
  • Accidental payments are non-refundable
  • Technical failures require proof for review

For Payment Issues:

Email info@staxtech.in with transaction details. Valid cases will be reviewed within 7 business days.
