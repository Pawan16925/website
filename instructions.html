StaxTech Internship Instructions

Step 1: Enrollment

Successfully fill the StaxTech Internship Program form to complete your enrollment.

Step 2: Offer Letter (Within 24 Hours)

You will receive your Offer Letter within 24 hours on your registered Gmail. Please also check your Spam folder.

If you do not receive the email, visit:
https://staxtech.in/offer

  • View internship details
  • Download your Offer Letter

Step 3: First Task – LinkedIn Post

  • Post your Offer Letter on LinkedIn
  • Tag @staxtech.in
  • Mention your internship domain

This step is mandatory.

Step 4: Project Selection

Visit the project list on:
https://staxtech.in/offer

  • Record a short project demo/explanation video

Step 6: LinkedIn Project Post

  • Post your project video on LinkedIn
  • Tag @staxtech.in
  • Include project name, technologies used, and GitHub link

Step 7: Final Project Submission

Submit your project to receive the Internship Completion Certificate:

https://staxtech.in/project-submission

  • Fill all required details carefully
  • Provide correct GitHub and LinkedIn links
